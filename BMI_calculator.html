Understanding BMI

What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight. It's a useful screening tool to identify potential weight problems, though it doesn't directly measure body fat.

Underweight

BMI less than 18.5

Normal Weight

BMI 18.5 - 24.9

Overweight

BMI 25 - 29.9

Obese

BMI 30 or higher
